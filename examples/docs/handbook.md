# Acme Field Guide

## Refund policy
Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days,
refunds are issued as store credit only. Refunds are processed within 5 business days.

## Support hours
Support is available Monday through Friday, 8am to 6pm Pacific time.
Priority customers can reach the on-call line 24/7.

## Data retention
Customer data is retained for 7 years for audit purposes and then deleted.
